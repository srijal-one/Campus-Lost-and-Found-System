# Problem Statement
There's no easy, all-in-one system on campus for students to log and track lost or found items. We mostly rely on clunky notice boards, random Whatsapp groups, or word of mouth,but these ways are pretty disorganized. It's hard to search, and items aren't tracked properly. My projects tackles this, by offering a light weight menu based system. You can report, search and update in lost and found items all in one place, for as long as the session runs.

# Scope of The project 
My whole system cover the whole lost and found management system flow:
1. We can report lost item with its name and location.
2. We can report found item as same.
3. Can view all the lost and found items with their current status.
4. Search for any item.
5. Mark an item Returned once it is handover to its owner.
6. Delete a wrong or duplicate entered items.

This project use the concepts of functions, lists, loops, conditional statements and functional modules. Data handling ( permanent storage to entered data) , user authentication are considered for future development of this project.

# Target Users

1. With this project, my main targeted users are students who have lost their items or they have found some item of someone's in the college campus and they want to report or search for that item .
2. Secondly Are the Campus staff members who take of these things can use this to maintain the records and handing the items to their right owners.


# High Level Features
1. You can report a lost item or found item with its name and place.
2. View all the lost and found items at once separately in their respective menus.
3. Search for any item across lost and found items list.
4. Change the item status to "Returned" if handover to its owner.
5. Delete incorrect or duplicate entries from the system.
6. Simple numbered menu for easy navigation of features and invalid option choosen message if choice selection is wrong.
